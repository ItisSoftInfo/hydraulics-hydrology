37. Flow over a Hump: The Surface Drops in Slow Flow, Rises in Fast Flow

Change the yellow boxes and the results follow.

Inputs (yellow boxes)
channel width bm
flow Qm³/s
depth upstream y1m
hump height Δzm
trial depth on the hump (Solve changes it)m
Results
flow per meter qm²/s
q = Q / b
critical depth ycm
yc = (q² / 9.81)^(1 / 3)
least energy Emin = 1.5 ycm
Emin = 1.5 × yc
Froude number upstream
Froude number upstream = q / y1 / √(9.81 × y1)
E upstreamm
E upstream = y1 + q² / (2 × 9.81 × y1²)
E on the hump = E1 − Δzm
hump = E upstream − Δz
E at the trial depthm
E at the trial depth = hump + q² / (2 × 9.81 × hump²)
difference (make 0)m
difference = E at the trial depth − hump
Froude number on the hump
hump = q / hump / √(9.81 × hump)
change of the surface = y2 + Δz − y1m
change of the surface = hump + Δz − y1
highest hump without choking = E1 − Eminm
highest hump without choking = E upstream − Emin
Start above yc for slow flow, below yc for fast flow.
