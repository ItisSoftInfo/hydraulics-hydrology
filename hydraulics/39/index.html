Narrowing
Inputs (yellow boxes)
canal width b1m
flow Qm³/s
depth in the canal y1m
width of the opening b2m
sill height in the openingm
trial depth in the opening (Solve changes it)m
Results
flow per meter in the canal q1m²/s
q1 = Q / b1
Froude number in the canal
Froude number in the canal = q1 / y1 / √(9.81 × y1)
E in the canalm
E in the canal = y1 + q1² / (2 × 9.81 × y1²)
flow per meter in the opening q2m²/s
q2 = Q / b2
least energy of the opening 1.5 ycm
yc = 1.5 × (q2² / 9.81)^(1 / 3)
E available on the sillm
sill = E in the canal − sill height in the opening
the flow
flow = if sill ≥ yc: “passes”; otherwise “chokes”
narrowest width without a sillm
sill = Q / (√(9.81) × (2 × E in the canal / 3)^1.5)
E at the trial depth − E available (make 0)m
E at the trial depth − E available = trial depth in the opening + q2² / (2 × 9.81 × trial depth in the opening²) − sill
change of the surface = y2 + sill − y1m
change of the surface = trial depth in the opening + sill height in the opening − y1
Froude number in the opening
Froude number in the opening = q2 / trial depth in the opening / √(9.81 × trial depth in the opening)
Start above yc for slow flow.
