flow Qm³/s
bed slope S
bottom width bm
side slope z (z across : 1 up)
safety factor SF
freeboardm
liningbare claygravel 50 mmrock 0.15 mconcrete
Manning n
permissible shear τp (N/m²)rigid
trial depth y (m), Solve changes it
flow area A (m²)
A = (b + z × y) × y
gravel 50 mm = (b + z × y) × y
rock 0.15 m = (b + z × y) × y
rigid = (b + z × y) × y
wetted perimeter P (m)
P = b + 2 × y × √(1 + z²)
gravel 50 mm = b + 2 × y × √(1 + z²)
rock 0.15 m = b + 2 × y × √(1 + z²)
rigid = b + 2 × y × √(1 + z²)
hydraulic radius R (m)
R = A / P
gravel 50 mm = A / P
rock 0.15 m = A / P
rigid = A / P
flow Q at the trial depth (m³/s)
flow Q at the trial depth = 1 / n × R^(2 / 3) × √(S) × A
gravel 50 mm = 1 / n × R^(2 / 3) × √(S) × A
rock 0.15 m = 1 / n × R^(2 / 3) × √(S) × A
rigid = 1 / n × R^(2 / 3) × √(S) × A
difference Q − flow (make 0)
flow = flow Q at the trial depth − Q
gravel 50 mm = flow Q at the trial depth − Q
rock 0.15 m = flow Q at the trial depth − Q
rigid = flow Q at the trial depth − Q
velocity V (m/s)
V = Q / A
gravel 50 mm = Q / A
rock 0.15 m = Q / A
rigid = Q / A
design shear τd = γ y S (N/m²)
τd = 9810 × y × S
gravel 50 mm = 9810 × y × S
rock 0.15 m = 9810 × y × S
rigid = 9810 × y × S
needed: SF × τd (N/m²)
needed = SF × τd
gravel 50 mm = SF × τd
rock 0.15 m = SF × τd
rigid = SF × τd
verdict
verdict = if τp is a number: if τp ≥ needed: “passes”; otherwise “fails”; otherwise “no shear limit”
gravel 50 mm = if τp is a number: if τp ≥ needed: “passes”; otherwise “fails”; otherwise “no shear limit”
rock 0.15 m = if τp is a number: if τp ≥ needed: “passes”; otherwise “fails”; otherwise “no shear limit”
rigid = if τp is a number: if τp ≥ needed: “passes”; otherwise “fails”; otherwise “no shear limit”
average shear γ R S (not for the check)
S = 9810 × R × S
gravel 50 mm = 9810 × R × S
rock 0.15 m = 9810 × R × S
rigid = 9810 × R × S
depth with freeboard (m)
depth with freeboard = y + freeboard
gravel 50 mm = y + freeboard
rock 0.15 m = y + freeboard
rigid = y + freeboard
γ = 9,810 N/m³. Linings, n and permissible shear: US FHWA HEC-15 (2005), Tables 2.1 to 2.3, public domain.
