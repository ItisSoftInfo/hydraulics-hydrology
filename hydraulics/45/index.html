difference (make 0)m³/s
difference = Q by Manning at the trial depth − Q
critical depth ycm
yc = (q² / 9.81)^(1 / 3)
Froude number at normal depth
Froude number at normal depth = q / trial normal depth / √(9.81 × trial normal depth)
critical slope Sc (normal depth = yc)
Sc = (n × q / yc)² / (b × yc / (b + 2 × yc))^(4 / 3)
the slope is
the slope is = if trial normal depth > yc: “mild: yn above yc”; otherwise “steep: yn below yc”
