42. Energy Lost in a Hydraulic Jump, Kinds of Jumps and Stilling Basins

Change the yellow boxes and the results follow.

Energy loss
Inputs (yellow boxes)
channel width bm
flow Qm³/s
depth before the jump y1m
Results
flow per meter qm²/s
q = Q / b
Froude number Fr1
Fr1 = q / y1 / √(9.81 × y1)
depth after the jump y2m
y2 = y1 / 2 × (√(1 + 8 × Fr1²) − 1)
specific energy before E1m
E1 = y1 + q² / (2 × 9.81 × y1²)
specific energy after E2m
E2 = y2 + q² / (2 × 9.81 × y2²)
head lost ΔE = (y2 − y1)³ / (4 y1 y2)m
ΔE = (y2 − y1)³ / (4 × y1 × y2)
check: E1 − E2m
E2 = E1 − E2
share of E1 lost
lost = ΔE / E1
power lost = γ Q ΔEkW
lost = 9.81 × Q × ΔE
kind of jump (Froude number ranges, USBR)
jump = if Fr1 < 1: “no jump”; if Fr1 < 1.7: “undular”; if Fr1 < 2.5: “weak”; if Fr1 < 4.5: “oscillating”; if Fr1 < 9: “steady”; otherwise “strong”
Stilling basin
Inputs (yellow boxes)
channel width bm
flow Qm³/s
jet depth on the original floorm
tailwater depth above the original floorm
margin on the depth after the jump
trial jet depth on the lowered floor (Solve changes it)m
Results
flow per meter qm²/s
q = Q / b
E of the jet on the original floorm
E of the jet on the original floor = jet depth on the original floor + q² / (2 × 9.81 × jet depth on the original floor²)
first estimate: lower the floor by margin × y2 − tailwaterm
first estimate: lower the floor = jump × jet depth on the original floor / 2 × (√(1 + 8 × (q / jet depth on the original floor / √(9.81 × jet depth on the original floor))²) − 1) − tailwater depth above the original floor
E of the trial jetm
jet = trial jet depth on the lowered floor + q² / (2 × 9.81 × trial jet depth on the lowered floor²)
floor drop that gives this jetm
jet = jet − E of the jet on the original floor
y2 of the trial jetm
jet = trial jet depth on the lowered floor / 2 × (√(1 + 8 × (q / trial jet depth on the lowered floor / √(9.81 × trial jet depth on the lowered floor))²) − 1)
depth needed = margin × y2m
depth needed = jump × jet
depth available = tailwater + floor dropm
depth available = tailwater depth above the original floor + jet
difference (make 0)m
difference = depth available − depth needed
