flow per meter qm²/s
q = Q / b
jet depth y1 = Cc am
y1 = Cc × a
Froude number of the jet
jet = q / y1 / √(9.81 × y1)
sequent depth of the jet y2*m
sequent depth of the jet y2* = y1 / 2 × (√(1 + 8 × jet²) − 1)
Q by Manning at the trial tailwaterm³/s
Q by Manning at the trial tailwater = b × trial tailwater depth / Manning n of the canal below × (b × trial tailwater depth / (b + 2 × trial tailwater depth))^(2 / 3) × √(bed slope of the canal below)
difference (make 0)m³/s
difference = Q by Manning at the trial tailwater − Q
where the jump forms
where the jump forms = if trial tailwater depth < sequent depth of the jet y2* − 0.01: “repelled: further down”; if trial tailwater depth > sequent depth of the jet y2* + 0.01: “drowned: against the gate”; otherwise “right at the jet”

Results
flow per meter qm²/s
q = Q / b
critical depth ycm
yc = (q² / 9.81)^(1 / 3)
chute: Q by Manning − Q (make 0)m³/s
Q = b × trial normal depth on the chute / n × (b × trial normal depth on the chute / (b + 2 × trial normal depth on the chute))^(2 / 3) × √(slope of the chute) − Q
canal: Q by Manning − Q (make 0)m³/s
Q = b × trial normal depth on the canal / n × (b × trial normal depth on the canal / (b + 2 × trial normal depth on the canal))^(2 / 3) × √(slope of the canal) − Q
chute
chute = if trial normal depth on the chute < yc: “steep”; otherwise “mild”
canal
canal = if trial normal depth on the canal < yc: “steep”; otherwise “mild”
sequent depth of the chute's normal flowm
flow = trial normal depth on the chute / 2 × (√(1 + 8 × (q / trial normal depth on the chute / √(9.81 × trial normal depth on the chute))²) − 1)
where the jump forms
where the jump forms = if trial normal depth on the canal > flow: “on the chute: S1 curve up to the break”; otherwise “on the canal, after an M3 curve”
