Expansion
Inputs (yellow boxes)
small diameter D1m
large diameter D2m
flow QL/s
cone factor (share of the sudden loss)
water density ρkg/m³
Results
velocity V1m/s
V1 = Q / 1000 / (π × D1² / 4)
velocity V2m/s
V2 = Q / 1000 / (π × D2² / 4)
area ratio A1/A2
area ratio = (D1 / D2)²
loss (V1 − V2)²/2gm
loss = (V1 − V2)² / (2 × 9.81)
K1 = (1 − A1/A2)²
K1 = (1 − area ratio)²
K1 × V1²/2gm
K1 × V1²/2g = K1 × V1² / (2 × 9.81)
K2 = (A2/A1 − 1)²
K2 = (1 / area ratio − 1)²
K2 × V2²/2gm
K2 × V2²/2g = K2 × V2² / (2 × 9.81)
pressure rise without losskPa
loss = ρ × (V1² − V2²) / 2 / 1000
loss as pressure ρ g hLkPa
hL = ρ × 9.81 × loss / 1000
pressure rise with the losskPa
loss = loss − hL
check, momentum: ρ V2 (V1 − V2)kPa
V2 = ρ × V2 × (V1 − V2) / 1000
cone: lossm
loss = cone factor × loss
cone: pressure risekPa
rise = loss − ρ × 9.81 × loss / 1000
The cone factor 0.2 is an example value; real cones depend on their angle (use the maker's value).
