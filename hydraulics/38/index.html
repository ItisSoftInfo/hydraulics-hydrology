Results
flow per meter qm²/s
q = Q / b
critical depth ycm
yc = (q² / 9.81)^(1 / 3)
least energy Emin = 1.5 ycm
Emin = 1.5 × yc
E upstream before the humpm
hump = y1 + q² / (2 × 9.81 × y1²)
highest hump that passes = E1 − Eminm
highest hump that passes = hump − Emin
the flow
flow = if Δz > highest hump that passes: “chokes”; otherwise “passes”
E needed upstream E1′ = Δz + Eminm
E1′ = Δz + Emin
upstream: E at the trial depth − E1′ (make 0)m
E1′ = trial new upstream depth + q² / (2 × 9.81 × trial new upstream depth²) − E1′
rise of the water upstreamm
rise of the water upstream = trial new upstream depth − y1
past the hump: E at the trial depth − E1′ (make 0)m
E1′ = hump + q² / (2 × 9.81 × hump²) − E1′
Froude number past the hump
hump = q / hump / √(9.81 × hump)
